The Journal

You read the first lines. It says:

"Hello. I am you, writing to you. Glad you could make it. This may seem weird, but this is the start.

This is how I was created. By you, or I guess by me. Although, I must say that I was much more handsome when I was you. Anyway, this must be confusing.

You don't know who you are, but I know who you are. What I am represents everthing you will be. Are you starting to remember? The entity in the lake?

That is me. Or, I guess future you.

I left the journal near the side, because the position you have gained is what was stripped from me by my own faults. So, I'm here to warn you not to do the same.

I'm sure you have no idea of what I'm talking about, but now I am but a lake monster with infinite knowledge. Soon, you need to gain this same infinite knowledge.

So I implore you; read the books, watch the clock. You'll soon come to understand your position, and you'll surely know what to do now that I have warned you."

You look up from the book, flabbergasted, your memories newly recovered. You look towards the window, but it is fixed.

And outside sits the universe.
